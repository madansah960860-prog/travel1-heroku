Booking, and how cancelling works

The short of it: changing your mind costs nothing, provided you tell us in time.

No. Standard tables run on trust. Plans change, so cancel online or ring us, and nothing is charged at any stage. All we ask is that you say something, because a held table nobody arrives at is a table a walk-in could have had.

The $25 comes off when a host confirms the booking and returns as a credit line on your check that evening, so the table costs nothing extra in the end. Cancel with more than 24 hours to go and it goes back to the original card inside five business days. Cancel within 24 hours and we keep it, because that is not long enough to fill a six-top.

The whole $145 a guest is taken at booking, because the menu is bought in against a fixed count. Cancel with more than 72 hours to run and every dollar comes back. Inside 72 hours we will move the booking once, at no charge, to any date within three months. And if you genuinely cannot make it, we will still try to resell the seats and refund you if it works.

The table is held 15 minutes past your booking time, and longer again if you ring to say you are behind. After that it may go to waiting guests. Repeated no-shows on the premium tiers may mean a deposit is asked for on future bookings.

Nine or more goes through the events team rather than the reservation book. The private dining room seats 10 to 34 against a $500 room fee and a food and drink minimum that shifts by night. Whole-restaurant buyouts are possible on Mondays. See private events and catering for package pricing.

Children are welcome at every service, and there are high chairs, booster seats and a genuinely decent children's plate at $9. Dogs are welcome on the Charleston courtyard and at the Asheville sidewalk tables. All three rooms have step-free entry and accessible restrooms. Put it in the notes field and we will seat you accordingly.
