Wages printed, not negotiated in the dark

Come and work here

Every role below carries its wage, its schedule and its real demands before you apply. Nobody should have to sit two interviews just to find out what a job pays.

What the job actually comes with

Pay

Front of house starts at $18 an hour before tips. Kitchen starts at $21. No tip credit is taken against wages in any state.

Health

Medical, dental and vision from 20 hours a week, the company carrying 70 percent of the employee premium.

Time

Two days off together, rostered two weeks ahead. Five company closure days a year, all paid.

Ownership

An employee ownership pool of 12 percent. Shares vest once you pass three years of continuous service.

Roles open now

Six positions across the three rooms, accurate as of this month. If none of them fit, write to us regardless: turnover is a fact of this trade, and good applications stay on file for six months.

Kitchen

Asheville and Nashville

Line Cook, Asheville

$21 to $25 per hour

Full time, four or five shifts a week, on hearth or garde manger depending where your strength lies.

  • Two years of professional kitchen experience preferred, not required
  • Shifts run 2:00 PM through 11:00 PM, with two days off together
  • You will learn butchery whether or not you asked to

Baker, Asheville

$23 to $26 per hour

Full time, five shifts. Every loaf for all three rooms is baked in this kitchen, so the day opens at 3:40 AM and closes at noon.

  • Sourdough experience required, we will not teach it from scratch in this role
  • Shift differential of $2 per hour for the early start
  • Home by lunch, every day

Sous Chef, Nashville

$62,000 to $70,000 per year

Salaried, holding the pass four nights a week along with the hardwood grill programme. Reports to the head chef and works alongside Marisol on the weekly card.

  • Live fire cooking experience essential
  • Ordering and stock sit with you for the meat programme
  • Relocation support of up to $3,000 available

Front of house and support

all three locations

Server, Charleston

$18 per hour plus tips

Part time or full time. Tips pool across the entire shift, kitchen included, and work out at an extra $12 to $19 an hour over a month.

  • No experience needed, we train the menu properly
  • You will be expected to know where every ingredient came from
  • Courtyard shifts March to November

Bartender, Nashville

$19 per hour plus tips

Full time, four shifts taking in both weekend nights. This is our biggest bar, and its zero proof list is ordered just as often as the cocktails.

  • Two years behind a bar required
  • You will help build the seasonal list rather than simply pour it
  • A Tennessee alcohol server permit is needed within 30 days

Produce Driver and Buyer's Assistant, Asheville

$20 per hour

Three mornings a week, 5:30 AM through 11:30 AM. You take the Transit out, collect from as many as nine farms, and check every crate before it is loaded.

  • Clean driving licence required, we insure the vehicle
  • Physical work, crates run to 40 pounds
  • The best route into this company if sourcing is what you want to learn
