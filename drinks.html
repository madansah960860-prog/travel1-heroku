The bar list

Twenty pours over five sections. The cocktails follow the orchard and the herb bed, the wine list is short by choice, and the zero proof section gets the same work as everything else.

  • Cocktails
  • Beer and cider
  • Wine
  • Zero proof
  • Coffee and tea
GF Gluten free as poured
VG Vegan
N Contains nuts
Bar pick Chosen by our bar lead, Nadia Okonkwo

Alcohol is served only to guests of 21 and over, and anybody who looks under 40 will be carded. A handful of wines and ciders are fined with egg white or fish products. Ask, and we will go through the producer's notes for you. See the allergen disclaimer.

Cocktails

built on the herb bed out back

Twelve Mile Old Fashioned

$16

Cask strength Tennessee bourbon, muscadine syrup in place of sugar, and bitters we tincture from black walnut hulls gathered in October.

  • Stirred 40 turns over one hand-cut cube
  • Muscadine leaves a darker, grassier finish than demerara would
  • Contains tree nuts from the walnut bitters
GFNBar pick

Orchard House Sour

$15

Apple brandy, lemon, honey off the hives on the Charleston roof, and a float of dry cider from Noble Hard Cider in Waynesville.

  • Shaken hard, no egg white, and it still foams off the cider
  • Honey changes character three times a year
  • Ask for it long over ice in summer
GF

Herb Bed Gimlet

$14

Gin, a lime cordial built on the whole fruit, and whichever soft herb is running hardest in the bed that week: mint, shiso or lemon balm.

  • Herb of the week is chalked on the bar
  • Cordial uses whole limes so nothing is wasted
  • The lightest drink on the list
VGGF

Smoked Pepper Paloma

$15

Blanco tequila, grapefruit, lime and a syrup off peppers we smoke and dry here. Salted rim if you want one.

  • Heat sits at a steady medium, not a dare
  • Peppers come from Rivercane Gardens
  • Half the salt on request, or none at all
VGGF

Vineyard Spritz

$13

Muscadine wine out of the Yadkin Valley, a bitter aperitif, soda and a wide strip of orange peel.

  • Low alcohol, roughly 8 percent by volume
  • Muscadine is the native Southern grape
  • Good before dinner, poor after it
VGGF

Beer and cider

eight taps, all Southeastern

Burial Beer Co. Pilsner, Asheville NC

$7

Clean German-style pilsner, 4.8 percent, poured into a 16 ounce glass. Brewed two miles from the Asheville door.

  • The staff drink of choice after a double
  • Pitcher of 60 ounces for $24

Westbrook Gose, Mount Pleasant SC

$8

Salted sour wheat beer, 4 percent, sharp enough to cut the richer plates off the hearth.

  • Order it alongside the pork chop
  • Contains wheat and coriander

Noble Dry Hard Cider, Waynesville NC

$9

Bone dry cider pressed off mountain apples, 6.5 percent, carrying real tannin out of the fruit instead of added acid.

  • Naturally gluten free
  • Bottle of 750ml for $28
GFVG

Yazoo Dos Perros Brown Ale, Nashville TN

$8

Malty American brown ale, 3.5 percent, on tap in Nashville alone. The right thing beside anything off the hardwood grill.

  • Nashville dining room only
  • Session strength, order two

Wine

deliberately short, mostly low intervention

Chenin Blanc, Vouvray, Loire Valley 2022

$14 glass / $54 bottle

Dry, waxy and mineral, carrying just enough weight for the trout and the cabbage. The most versatile bottle we stock.

  • Poured in a 6 ounce measure
  • Vegan, no animal fining agents used
VG

Cabernet Franc, Yadkin Valley, North Carolina 2021

$15 glass / $58 bottle

Grown 120 miles out from Asheville. Green pepper on the nose, red fruit sitting under it, and tannin light enough to take twenty minutes in the fridge.

  • The closest wine to our own radius
  • Served at cellar temperature, not room
Bar pick

Skin Contact Pinot Gris, Willamette Valley 2022

$16 glass / $62 bottle

Ten days on the skins leaves it apricot coloured with a grippy finish. Made for the shellfish pot and the onion tart.

  • Unfiltered, so expect some sediment
  • Never had an orange wine? Begin with this one
VG

Gamay, Beaujolais Villages 2023

$13 glass / $50 bottle

Light, bright, served cold. It is the bottle we pull for staff meal, which should tell you plenty.

  • Works with everything on the garden section
  • Half bottles available for $27
VG

Zero proof

same effort, no alcohol

Blackberry Shrub and Soda

$9

Blackberries left in cider vinegar and sugar for a fortnight, then stretched out with soda and a great deal of ice.

  • Sharp, not sweet, and genuinely refreshing
  • That shrub base is sold on the pantry shelf too
VGGF

Fennel and Grapefruit Tonic

$10

Fennel seed cordial, fresh grapefruit and a dry tonic. Bitter enough to stand up as a real aperitif.

  • Contains quinine from the tonic
  • Ask for it unsweetened and the build gets adjusted
VGGF

House Water Kefir, Mint and Cucumber

$8

Lightly fizzy water kefir, taken through a second ferment with mint and cucumber. Trace alcohol under 0.5 percent, as in any live ferment.

  • Brewed in the Asheville basement, bottled weekly
  • Not for you if you avoid alcohol altogether
VGGF

Coffee and tea

roasted for us in Asheville

Embervine House Filter Coffee

$5

A washed Colombian and Ethiopian blend, roasted for us in small batches. Brewed by the litre, refilled at no charge.

  • Refills are free the whole time you are sitting
  • Whole beans at the host stand, a 12 ounce bag for $18
VGGF

Drying Rack Tisane, Pot for Two

$9

Whatever came off the drying racks at the end of last season: lemon balm, tulsi, chamomile and a little dried apple. Caffeine free.

  • Blend is chalked at the host stand each month
  • Steeps twice, second pot of water is free
VGGF

Pairings, minus the performance

Ask any server for a pairing and you get a straight answer at three price points rather than a rehearsed script. If a $13 glass sits better with your plate than a $58 bottle, that is exactly what you will be told.

Chef's Table guests can add the wine and cider pairing at $75 a person, or the zero proof pairing of shrubs, ferments and teas at $45.
