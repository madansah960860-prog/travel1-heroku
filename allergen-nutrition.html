Allergens and nutrition, stated plainly

Last updated August 12, 2026. Read this before you rely on any allergen or nutrition information on this site or on our menus. It sets out what can be promised, what we cannot, and how to give yourself the best chance of a safe meal here.

The part that matters most

Wheat, dairy, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame all pass through our kitchens, in shared spaces and on shared equipment. No dish can be guaranteed free of any allergen. If your allergy is severe, ask for a manager before ordering, so you can be told honestly whether we are able to feed you safely that night.

2. The nine major allergens, and where each one lives in our kitchens

United States law names nine major food allergens. Every one of the nine is present in at least one of our kitchens.

3. Why no allergen free plate will ever be promised here

One kitchen turns out every dish. Across a service, pans, boards, fryers, grills, cloths and hands all move between preparations. Flour lifts into the air during the bake and settles on everything. Ingredients come from suppliers whose own facilities are outside our control, and any of them can change a formulation without telling us first.

A restaurant promising you a guaranteed allergen free dish out of a shared kitchen is either using that word loosely or has not thought it through. We would rather say it straight: enormous care will be taken, the process will be changed for you wherever it can be, and we will still not promise an outcome that is not ours to control.

4. What we actively do instead

  • Allergy details taken at booking or on an order print onto a ticket in the kitchen, rather than stopping at the host stand.
  • Every dish carries a written ingredient specification, revised whenever a supplier changes.
  • Ask, and your plate gets a freshly cleaned board, a clean pan, fresh oil and a service cloth of its own.
  • The dish will be run by a manager or the chef before it leaves the pass.
  • Where the judgement is that we cannot feed you safely, you will be told plainly rather than gambled with.
  • Servers are trained to fetch a manager instead of guessing. If one ever answers an allergy question with a guess, tell us, and it will be taken seriously.

5. Nutrition information

There are no calorie counts or full nutritional breakdowns published here, and the reason deserves stating. The card changes weekly around whatever the farms deliver, portion weights move because vegetables are not uniform, and a plate cooked to order is not a standardised industrial product. Numbers generated under those conditions would be guesswork wearing the costume of precision.

What you can have instead, at any point, is exactly what sits in a dish: every ingredient, the fats it is cooked in, the method and the rough portion size. Ask a server, or email hello@embervine.com ahead of time, and the current specification for any dish on the card will be sent over.

None of this site is medical or dietary advice. If you manage a medical condition through your diet, lean on your clinician or dietitian, and treat our ingredient information as material for that conversation rather than guidance in its own right.

6. Alcohol

A number of wines, ciders and beers are fined with egg white, casein, gelatin or isinglass, the last of which comes from fish. No US label is required to disclose it. We keep producer notes on most of the list and will check before you order. Our water kefir carries trace alcohol, as every live ferment does.

7. General website disclaimer

Everything on this site is published as general information about our rooms. Menus, prices, hours and ingredient details are kept as accurate and current as we can manage, but the food menu turns over weekly, so the card in the restaurant on a given night may not match the version here. Where the two disagree, the printed card in the room wins.

There are no affiliate links on this site and no commission reaches us from anything you click away to. Some pages carry clearly labelled advertising; those adverts come from third parties and are not endorsements from us. Nothing here is professional, medical, legal or financial advice.

8. Speak to us before you come in

The most useful thing you can possibly do is tell us early. Put the detail in the notes field as you book, or ring the room directly. A kitchen holding that information at 3:00 PM can plan around it properly. A kitchen learning it at 8:15 PM in the middle of a full service has far less room to work with.
