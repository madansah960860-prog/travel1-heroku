Takeout and delivery

Order on this page and you pay the menu price. Nothing for service, nothing for packaging, nothing for ordering too little. Our own drivers run five miles out from each door for a flat $4.95, and further down we are straight with you about what the third-party apps cost you instead.

Pickup

Menu price, and nothing on top

Pick a 15 minute window. Two bays outside every restaurant are held for collection, and if you would rather stay put we will walk the bag out to the car.

Fee: $0.00

Our delivery

A flat $4.95 inside five miles

Run by our own staff in insulated boxes, so what leaves hot arrives hot. Every cent of a tip reaches the driver, and no tip is expected.

Fee: $4.95 flat, no distance surcharge

Delivery apps

There, but it will cost you more

We sit on the major apps because some guests would rather use them. The commission runs 15 to 30 percent, and their menu prices are lifted to cover it. Ordering here keeps that money out of the middle.

Fee: set by the app, not by us

Questions about ordering

Around 25 minutes for most orders placed away from peak service. Between 6:30 PM and 8:00 PM on a Friday or Saturday that can stretch to 45, and the confirmation text will say so rather than leaving you to work it out in the parking lot. Anything involving the clay pot chicken or the hog shoulder needs the notice periods printed on the card.

The sungold tomato toast softens inside ten minutes, the skillet cornbread surrenders its crust, and the shellfish pot is honestly not worth ordering to go. You will hear that at the point of ordering rather than pay for a dish that turns up worse than it left. Everything off the hearth, the grits and all four puddings travel perfectly well.

Yes. Twelve Mile Club points build on takeout and delivery at the same rate as the dining room, a point per dollar of food and drink, with a $25 credit landing at 250 points. Orders placed through third-party apps earn nothing, because those platforms never pass us your account details.

Beer, cider and wine can go onto delivery orders in North Carolina and Tennessee wherever our permits allow, and the driver checks government-issued photo identification at the door. With nobody aged 21 or over there to sign, the alcohol returns to the restaurant and is refunded. Cocktails do not travel at all.
