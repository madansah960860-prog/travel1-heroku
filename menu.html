The Chef's Table tasting

Eight seats look straight down the pass. Marisol and the sous chefs cook and plate an arm's length away, and talk through what arrived that morning and why it reached you in that particular shape.

$145 per guest for nine to eleven courses, roughly two and a half hours. Wine and cider pairing is an extra $75 per guest, or a non-alcoholic pairing of shrubs, teas and ferments for $45.

  • Wednesday through Saturday, a single seating at 6:30 PM
  • The whole table books as one, no split seatings
  • Dietary needs covered with 72 hours notice
  • Full prepayment at booking, refundable up to 72 hours before

Allergies and dietary needs

Tell your server before you order rather than after. Most dishes here can be adapted, and on the ones that cannot, you will get a straight answer instead of a guess. Detailed ingredient information is on the allergen and nutrition page.

What the price includes

The menu price is the whole food price. Sales tax goes on at the applicable state and local rate. There is no automatic service charge, no kitchen appreciation fee and no card surcharge. Gratuity is entirely your call.
